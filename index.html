SpellBlade

Welcome to the SpellBlade Alpha listbuilder. Below choose your faction, and optionally a guild if you wish to add another twist to your playstyle. Party Wide feats are available at all times, barring you can pay their cost. Heroes are special elite characters that are the core of your party, choose their gear and give them feats to excel in battle. Hirelings are your factions available warriors to hire for adventuring, they may be upgraded if you have the gold! Onec you are done, you can head to the battle mode and use that during play, or print out a PDF of your list.

Runs entirely in this browser tab — nothing is sent anywhere. Use Export/Import to save a ruleset or a list between sessions.
