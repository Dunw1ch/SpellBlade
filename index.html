A small slice of a larger list-builder. Choose a Faction and a Gold Cap for your list, then add Heroes — each built through Class, Guild, Weapons, Armor, Items, Feats, and Spells — plus any Hirelings your Faction allows. Every Hero and every Hireling counts against one shared gold budget. Switch to Admin Mode to add or edit the rules this builder reads from.

Runs entirely in this browser tab — nothing is sent anywhere. Use Export/Import to save a ruleset or a list between sessions.
